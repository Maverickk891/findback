How FindBack Operates

A structured 4-step framework bridging report submission to successful item return.

Stage 1
Report Item

Submit item details, date, category, and optional photographs. Distinguishing identifiers remain concealed.

Stage 2
Intelligent Match

TF-IDF vectorization and cosine similarity evaluate description (50%), category (20%), location (20%), and date (10%).

Stage 3
Verify Ownership

Claimants provide distinguishing proof. Finders or admins cross-verify secretly recorded markers before approval.

Stage 4
Secure Handover

Contact information is safely unlocked. Handover is completed and the item's status updates to Returned on the timeline.

Pre-Loss Protection

Prevent Loss with FindBack Smart QR Tags

Register your laptop, wallet, keys, or student ID beforehand. Attach the generated QR sticker. If you misplace it, any finder can scan it to send you an anonymous notification without ever seeing your phone number or email!
